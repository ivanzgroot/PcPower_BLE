Power button

Fires the relay across the PC power button, exactly as a real press would.

This holds the power button down for about six seconds. Most PCs cut power immediately, without saving anything. Use it only when the machine is unresponsive.

Reboot

Restarts the ESP32. Settings, learned devices and WiFi are kept.
